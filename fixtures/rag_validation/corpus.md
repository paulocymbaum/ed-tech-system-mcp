## Overview

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to convert light energy into chemical energy
stored as glucose. The process occurs mainly in chloroplasts and depends on chlorophyll
to absorb sunlight.

## Light-dependent reactions

The light-dependent reactions take place in the thylakoid membranes. During this stage,
water molecules are split, releasing oxygen as a byproduct. The energy captured from
light drives the production of ATP and NADPH.

## Calvin cycle

The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into organic molecules.
The end product includes glucose, which fuels plant growth and metabolism.

When retrieval works correctly, queries about photosynthesis should surface passages
mentioning chlorophyll, light-dependent reactions, and glucose.
